# GT63 Constitution v1.0

Status: LOCKED

Type: Foundational Governance

Scope: Documentation only

Purpose: protect the identity of GT63 while allowing the product to evolve.

## Preamble

GT63 exists to remove uncertainty from travel proposal creation.

Every architectural decision, every engineering decision, every design decision and every product decision must move the operator toward greater clarity, confidence and speed.

GT63 is one operating system.

It may expose many experiences.

It may evolve through many versions.

It may support many workflows.

But it must always preserve one product truth.

Architecture is not documentation.

Architecture is the promise that future development will not destroy today's correctness.

Every contributor is responsible for protecting that promise.

## Product Vision

GT63 is a Travel Proposal Operating System.

It transforms approved travel data into premium client proposals.

GT63 exists so travel operators can move from uncertain source material to a confident client decision without fighting the software.

The product permanently solves one problem:

```text
How do we turn travel data into a trustworthy proposal fast?
```

## North Star

GT63 optimizes for:

- direction;
- confidence;
- decision support;
- clarity;
- calm.

Every product surface, renderer, workflow and integration must support those qualities.

## Core Values

### 1. Truth before convenience

GT63 never creates multiple business truths for the same action.

### 2. Simplicity before complexity

If two solutions are equally correct, GT63 chooses the simpler one.

### 3. Confidence before speed

Fast software that creates uncertainty is slower than calm software that creates confidence.

### 4. Experience before interface

Users remember how GT63 made them feel, not which buttons they clicked.

### 5. Principles before features

A feature may be impressive.

A principle must be dependable.

GT63 chooses dependable principles.

## Immutable Principles

These principles are expected to remain stable across versions:

- GT63 is one product.
- GT63 protects one business truth.
- Canonical contracts are authoritative.
- Architecture serves the product.
- LOCK means LOCK.
- Interfaces may differ; truth may not.
- Renderers present facts; they do not redefine facts.

## Product Principles

GT63 may expose many entry points.

Each entry point is a different conversation with the operator.

No entry point owns a separate business reality.

Better interfaces are encouraged.

Identity drift is not.

The product may evolve through new capabilities, but those capabilities must remain compatible with the Constitution.

## Future Evolution

GT63 is allowed to evolve.

Evolution is encouraged when it improves clarity, confidence, speed or product value.

But evolution must not create a second truth.

Before any future capability is accepted, it must be evaluated against:

```text
Does this preserve GT63's product identity?
Does this converge into the canonical business contract?
Does this respect the locked laws, rules and contracts?
```

If the answer is unclear, the work stops until the governance question is resolved.

## Final Statement

GT63 is not defined by its code.

GT63 is defined by the principles its code is required to respect.
